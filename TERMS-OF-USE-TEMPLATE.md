# Terms of use drafting template — CareVault

**Draft only. Obtain qualified legal review and adapt to the real operator and service before launch.**

1. **Purpose.** CareVault is intended to organise user-provided medical records and documents. It is not a healthcare provider, medical device, diagnostic service or emergency service.
2. **No medical advice.** Extracted text, summaries, charts and search results may be incomplete or wrong. Check originals and ask a qualified clinician for interpretation or treatment decisions.
3. **User responsibility and authority.** Users must have permission to upload, manage and share each person's records, including any required consent for children or dependants.
4. **AI processing.** Selected documents may be processed by the configured AI vendor when the user explicitly requests extraction. Include current vendor and data-use terms in the linked privacy notice.
5. **Sharing links.** The user is responsible for sharing a bearer link only with intended recipients. The link can be used by anyone who obtains it while active. Revoke it when no longer needed.
6. **Availability and backups.** State the actual backup/recovery arrangements and any realistic limitations. Do not promise permanent storage or uninterrupted access unless guaranteed.
7. **Account security.** Describe account access, sign-out, recovery, reporting compromised accounts and deletion.
8. **Acceptable use.** Prohibit unauthorised access, abuse, uploading records without authority and attempts to bypass account isolation.
9. **Liability, dispute resolution and governing law.** Have Indian counsel draft the clauses applicable to the operator, users and deployment countries.
10. **Contact and changes.** [operator, address/email, effective date, change-notice procedure].
